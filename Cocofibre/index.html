Coconut fiber is natural fibers extracted from the outer coconut husk. The coir is located between the coconut shell and the outer layer of the coconut. There are two types of coconut fibers, the first coir, a coconut husk, which is recently harvested and used to make a finer brush, a rope, or a net. The second coir, a long-term husk from a tree, is generally lower than the previous coir. 

Coconut fiber also contains lignin that produces a low cellulose content and lightweight because cavities emerge from filter cells that have been dried. Thus, it makes it strong and lasting. We're here with an international quality coconut fiber product at your consistent supply of coconut fibers you need.

How Do We Make This Coconut Fiber

  1. The coconut husk is put to the conveyor then goes to the crusher engine to separate the fibers from the peat. The primary component of the wrecking machine is a spinning pit with a particular blade on it.
  2. Fibers from the crusher engine, break into the filter machine to separate the cocopeat and the cocofiber.
  3. The fibers that came out of the filter machine spread out in the dry floor and mixed it several times to separate some coco peats that were still attached.  
  4. The fibers are sun dried to make the water content is less than 18%.  
  5. After dry, the tested fibers are collected, put into a cleaning machine to remove short fibers and cocopeat for the last time.
  6. Then the fibers are compressed using the high-pressure press machine and plastic ropes to bind the stacks of cocofibers then ready to shipment.
