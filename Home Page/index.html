About Us

Seeing at the enormous potential outcomes of our agricultural land, we established to focus on any derivative coconut product, and to uphold these commodities to a higher level. We exist to boost the country’s foreign exchange and the economic value of an international quality coconut product at a price for the welfare of our farmers and customers. 

We are very sure, with the increased our country’s foreign exchange and empowerment support from us, the prosperity of local farmers will increase along with the quantity and quality of their products. With us, together we uphold the value of the Indonesian coconut.

OUR  PRODUCT 

Organic Coconut Sugar

Our natural organic coconut sugar is a premium natural sweetener devoid of added chemicals/additives. It is unbleached and contains no preservatives. Coconut Sugar, also sometimes referred to as palm sugar, is produced by evaporating the sap derived from the flowers of coconut trees.

Cocofiber

The cocofiber is the straw-like part of the coconut husk. ... It is a response to the high international market demand for coconut coir products, such as cocofiber and cocopeat for raw materials like spring beds, mattresses, car seats, ropes, and carpets.
